Prototype guide & definitions

Active opportunities = Qualified + Proposal. Follow-ups needed = non-Closed leads with a next follow-up on or before today in your browser’s local timezone. Overview and charts always show all leads. The follow-up calendar shows dated leads for the selected week or month and statuses, excluding Closed leads. Pipeline records status changes in this browser from the first tracking date; earlier history is unknown. Pipeline progress counts prospects entering each stage during the selected month. Closed is an ending status, not a confirmed sale. Pipeline history and work-hour logs are browser-local and separate from lead records. Work-time tracking is currently hidden; previously saved work logs are preserved. Calendar status buttons affect only the calendar; the table filters affect the table and CSV only.

Open a lead’s Edit button to view or change contact details, notes, product interest, and a fictional estimated value in USD. Blank value means unknown; zero is a valid estimate. These prototype fields are not approved company requirements. Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No backend or shared database. Avoid storing real customer information in this prototype. Export before resetting or clearing browser data.
